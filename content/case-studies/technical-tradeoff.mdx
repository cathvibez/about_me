## The constraint

_A real engineering constraint you had to make a product call inside of.
Latency against quality. Cost per request against accuracy. Build against buy.
A migration against a deadline. Name the numbers._

## The options

_Two or three genuine candidates, each with its cost. Show that you understood
the implementation well enough to price the alternatives — this is the block
that separates a technical PM from a PM who says they are technical._

## The call

_What you chose and the threshold that decided it. If you set an eval, a budget,
or an SLO, show it. A number you committed to in advance is worth more than a
paragraph of reasoning after the fact._

## What it cost

_Every real tradeoff has a bill. Name what you gave up and whether it turned out
to matter._

## What I would do differently

_Keep this one short. This study is the shortest of the three by design._
