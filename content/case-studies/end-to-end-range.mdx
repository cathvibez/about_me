## The bet

_Frame this as a thesis, not a hobby. What did you believe was true that the
market had not acted on yet? A side project described as "something I built for
fun" reads as a weekend toy; the same project described as a bet with a
hypothesis reads as product judgment._

## What I designed

_This is where the UI/UX claim gets proven. Show one screen and explain one
non-obvious decision in it — a flow you collapsed, a state you refused to add,
a default you argued about with yourself._

## What I built

_Keep this short and concrete. Stack, the one hard technical problem, and how you
solved it. You are proving the CS background is load-bearing, not listing tools._

## What happened

_Users, usage, or an honest "I shipped it and learned the thesis was wrong."
The second is a perfectly strong ending if you say what you learned._

## What I would do differently

_Same discipline as every other study._
